# Pyrolysis Furnace Intelligence

**Pyrolysis Furnace Intelligence** is a reduced-order engineering model and Windows desktop application for studying the coupled behaviour of an industrial steam-cracking furnace.

The model connects:

**Fuel & Combustion → Furnace Heat Transfer → Process Response**

It was developed from industrial furnace experience and first-principles engineering to make the cause-and-effect relationships between fuel quality, combustion, heat distribution and process behaviour easier to investigate.

## What the application examines

The application connects fuel composition and firing conditions with fuel properties, combustion air, flue-gas flow, CO2 production, furnace energy losses, radiant and convection duties, thermal efficiency, heat flux, coil pressure drop, hydrocarbon partial pressure, residence time and relative cracking severity.

The public model uses generalised engineering conditions and is intended for engineering study, sensitivity analysis and learning. It is not a plant digital twin, CFD package, BMS, APC, safety system or plant-authoritative operating tool.
